# Revenue-Intelligence-Dashboard-for-NovaTech-Solutions
In this project, you will build an end-to-end Revenue Intelligence Dashboard for NovaTech Solutions, a B2B SaaS company whose critical business data is spread across three separate systems. You will connect, transform, and visualize CRM deals, marketing campaigns, and support ticket data — then use AI-powered natural language querying to uncover insights that traditional dashboards alone might miss.

You are a newly hired Business Intelligence Analyst at NovaTech. The VP of Revenue Operations, Sarah Chen, needs her team to stop manually pulling reports from three different tools every Monday morning. She has asked you to unify the company's revenue data into a single interactive dashboard so the team can see the full customer journey — from marketing touchpoint to closed deal to support experience — in one place.

When complete, your Revenue Intelligence Dashboard will enable the revenue team to:
  Track marketing campaign performance and lead-to-deal conversion rates
  Monitor the sales pipeline by deal stage, segment, and win rate
  Identify at-risk accounts based on support ticket patterns
  Ask natural language questions across all three data domains using Quick Chat
  Share annotated, data-backed recommendations with executive stakeholders
  
After completing this project, you will be able to:
  Verify data quality across indexed knowledge bases by querying pre-indexed data through Quick Chat and comparing results against a data dictionary
  Transform and join datasets using no-code ETL workflows in the Data section, including calculated fields, type corrections, and multi-source joins
  Design and build a multi-page interactive dashboard with KPI cards, appropriate visual types, filters, drill-down, and cross-sheet navigation
  Configure and use AI-powered natural language querying by setting up a Topic in Quick Chat and performing exploratory analysis across multiple data domains
  Extract and communicate business insights to stakeholders through dashboard annotations and a written executive report

Your final submission includes the following artifacts:
  Verification log — at least 6 entries documenting data quality checks across all three knowledge bases
  Data transformation screenshots — join diagram, join configuration, calculated fields, and data type corrections
  Dashboard PDF export — all three sheets (Marketing Funnel, Sales Pipeline, Customer Health)
  Before/after Topic screenshots — showing Quick Chat responses before and after Topic configuration
  Q Exploration Log — at least 5 entries with questions, Q responses, and dashboard verification
  Dashboard executive summary — auto-generated from the published dashboard (if available)
  Annotated dashboard screenshots — 3–5 annotations with quantified findings and recommendations
  Written report for VP Sarah Chen — 1–3 pages covering data strategy, design rationale, key insights, and AI analysis comparison
